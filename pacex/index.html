Purpose

PACEX is a liquid potassium fertilizer with beneficial substances, formulated to support plant nutrition, vigor, and stress resilience in lawns, vegetables, flowers, shrubs, trees, and container plants.

Mixing & Handling Instructions

Shake well before use. Fill mix tank or container with half of the required water volume and begin agitation. Add products in this order, mixing thoroughly after each addition: adjuvants, pesticides, then fertilizers. Add remaining water and mix until uniform. Check chemical compatibility using a jar test prior to application.

Caution: Do not apply directly on or in contact with seed. Do not exceed recommended rates; salt injury or foliar burn may occur if over-applied.

Directions for Use

Plants / AreaMix or Rate
Lawns & TurfMix 12–16 oz in water and apply per 1,000 sq. ft. every 7–14 days.
Vegetables & FlowersMix 2–4 oz per gallon of water and apply to the soil every 1–2 weeks.
Trees, Shrubs & VinesMix 6–8 oz per gallon of water and apply to the root zone every 1–2 weeks.
Container & HouseplantsMix 1–2 oz per gallon of water and apply every 2–4 weeks.
Foliar FeedingDilute 1–2 oz per gallon of water and spray to lightly coat foliage every 1–2 weeks.

Test a small area first; avoid spraying in full sun or high heat. Do not exceed recommended rates.

Precautionary Statements

Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes, skin, and clothing. Avoid breathing spray mist or vapor. Wear protective eyewear and gloves when handling. Wash thoroughly after handling. Keep container closed.

First Aid

If in eyes: Hold eye open and rinse slowly and gently with water for 15–20 minutes. Remove contact lenses after the first 5 minutes, then continue rinsing. Call a poison control center or doctor for treatment advice.

If on skin or clothing: Take off contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with plenty of water for 15–20 minutes.

If swallowed: Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting unless directed by a poison control center or doctor. Call a poison control center or doctor.

If inhaled: Move person to fresh air.

Storage & Disposal

Do not contaminate water, food or feed by storage or disposal. Store away from incompatible materials. Dispose of contents/container in accordance with local authority requirements.

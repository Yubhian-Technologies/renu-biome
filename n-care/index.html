Purpose & General Information

Purpose: A liquid nitrogen plant food for home and garden use — vegetables (including tomatoes), flowers, lawns, trees, shrubs, fruit trees, and container plants — to promote strong, vigorous, deep-green growth.

N-Care is a clear, stabilized liquid nitrogen fertilizer. A nitrification inhibitor (dicyandiamide) slows the conversion of nitrogen in the soil, extending feeding and reducing loss. As a nitrogen-only plant food it is ideal for leafy growth and lawn green-up; for flowering and fruiting plants, use alongside a phosphorus and potassium source. N-Care may be blended with most phosphorus, potassium, and sulfur sources up to their solubility limits and works well in drip and hose-end systems. Do not mix with strong acids, as the product will degrade and precipitate.

Directions for Use

Shake well before using. Dilute the concentrate in water as shown below and apply to the soil or root zone — not to dry, wilted, or heat-stressed plants. Water in lightly after applying.

Recommendations & Best Practices

  • Apply in the early morning or evening; avoid feeding during the heat of the day.
  • Do not over-apply — too much nitrogen can burn foliage and roots and force weak, leggy growth.
  • For blooms and fruit, pair N-Care with a phosphorus/potassium fertilizer; alone it mainly drives green, leafy growth.
  • Run a small jar test before tank-mixing with any pesticide, herbicide, or fungicide.
  • Keep away from strong acids.

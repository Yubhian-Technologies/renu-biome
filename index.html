Purpose & General Information

Purpose: A liquid potassium plant food with seaweed for home and garden use — lawns, vegetables (including tomatoes), flowers, trees, shrubs, vines, fruit trees, and container plants — that feeds both plants and soil life for strong, vigorous growth and improved stress resilience.

Biome Care pairs readily available potassium and nitrogen with Ascophyllum nodosum seaweed extract, which serves as a food source for beneficial soil microbes. It disperses readily in most fertilizer and micronutrient mixes and can be applied with a watering can, hose-end sprayer, or drip and irrigation systems.

Shake well before use.

Directions for Use

Shake well before using. Dilute in water as shown below and apply to the soil and root zone. Water in after applying. Do not exceed the recommended rates.

Plants / AreaMix or RateHow Often
Lawns & turf6–10 oz in water per 1,000 sq. ft.Every 2–3 weeks

Recommendations & Best Practices

  • Shake well before each use — the seaweed fraction can settle between applications.
  • Apply in the early morning or evening, and avoid spraying in full sun or high heat.
  • Water in after soil applications to carry nutrients down to the root zone.
  • Do not over-apply — salt injury or foliar burn can occur if recommended rates are exceeded.
  • Feeds soil microbes as well as plants; reapply through the growing season for best results.
  • Run a small jar test for compatibility before tank-mixing with other products.

Caution

WARNING — KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.

Causes skin irritation and serious eye irritation. Avoid breathing mist, spray, or vapor. Wear eye protection and protective gloves; use only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area; wash thoroughly after handling.

First Aid — If in eyes: rinse cautiously with water for several minutes; remove contact lenses if present and continue rinsing. If on skin: wash with plenty of water; take off contaminated clothing. If irritation persists, get medical advice/attention.

Storage & Disposal

Store in a cool, well-ventilated place away from incompatible materials; keep the container tightly closed and protect from freezing. Do not contaminate water, food, or feed by storage or disposal. Dispose of contents and container in accordance with local authority requirements.

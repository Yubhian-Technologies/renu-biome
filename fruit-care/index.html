General Information

Fruit Care 60% Calcium has been specifically formulated to help with Calcium deficiencies. This product is an aqueous suspension containing Calcium Carbonate. Fruit Care 60% Calcium may be applied alone, with pesticides, or with other fertilizers to the soil or foliage using ground sprayers, aircraft, and fertigation systems.

Directions for Use

Ground: Apply 1–2 gallons per acre. It is recommended that Fruit Care 60% Calcium is applied to actively growing plants with young leaves be sprayed immediately following irrigation or rainfall for maximum foliar uptake. Thorough coverage and wetting are crucial to optimize foliar spray results. Agitate contents before use and keep from freezing. Apply with an adequate amount of water that will provide complete coverage. Do not apply undiluted. The rate of application will depend on the crop and stage of growth. Do not use on crops where a visible white residue cannot be tolerated or washed off.

Aerial Sprays: Use up to 3–5 gallons per acre. However, more dilute solutions produce more coverage and results.

Compatibility & Caution

Fruit Care 60% Calcium is compatible with most liquid fertilizers, insecticides, fungicides, and herbicides. Use in accordance with recommendations of a qualified individual or institution such as, but not limited to, a certain crop extension publication, or apply according to recommendations in your approved nutrient management plan.

Caution: When tank mixing other products a "jar test" is recommended. Possible precipitation may be experienced when combined with materials containing phosphorus or sulfate, when the pH of the tank water is above 6.5, or if insufficient water volumes are used.

Caution & First Aid

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.

Harmful if swallowed. Causes mild skin irritation. Causes eye irritation.

(GHS-US): Avoid breathing mist, spray, vapors. Wash face and hands thoroughly after handling. Use only outdoors or in a well-ventilated area. Wear eye protection, face protection, protective gloves. Based on currently available data, this product is not classified as a GHS hazardous substance. However, observe good industrial hygiene practices.

If on skin: Wash with plenty of water. If inhaled: Remove person to fresh air and keep comfortable for breathing. If in eyes: Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing. Call a doctor if you feel unwell. If skin/eye irritation persists: Get medical advice/attention. Take off contaminated clothing and wash before reuse. Store in a well-ventilated place.
